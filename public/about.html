About the author

Rameshkumar (Ramesh) Varanganti

Enterprise Integration Lead and Architect with 20+ years of SAP integration experience, independent researcher, and creator of Agentic Integration Hub.

Integration architecture, from SAP PI to AI agents

I have spent more than two decades designing and running enterprise integrations, and I built this site to document how that discipline changes when AI agents start calling governed APIs and MCP servers.

My work centres on SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) Integration Suite: Cloud Integration (CPI, formerly HCI), API Management, Event Mesh and Integration Advisor, along with SAP Process Integration / Process Orchestration (PI/PO), Groovy scripting, and B2B/EDI integration. I have delivered integration landscapes across manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, petroleum and distribution.

In my current role I am the end-to-end point of contact for SAP Cloud Integration across a large global workforce, covering HR and workforce systems as well as ERP and commercial processes. That day-to-day ownership of production interfaces, from design and security through monitoring and incident response, is what shapes the practical, operations-first tone of the guides here.

What I write about

  • Agentic integration: connecting enterprise AI agents to ERP, HR, CRM and finance systems safely
  • MCP gateways and servers in front of existing APIs and integration flows
  • API-centric integration, API management and event-driven architecture
  • Zero Trust, least privilege and policy governance for automated and agent-driven processes
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and copilots on SAP BTP
