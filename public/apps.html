Before you use a plugin

  • Use synthetic or redacted examples. Never paste passwords, tokens, private keys, personal employee data or confidential payloads.
  • Review generated scripts, mappings and configuration, and test in a non-production tenant first.
  • Your AI host processes your prompts under its own terms and privacy policy.
