Privacy Policy

For the agenticintegrationhub skills-only plugin. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Current release: agenticintegrationhub supplies instructions and reference material for enterprise agent integration, MCP, gateways, and tool routing. It does not include a publisher-operated MCP endpoint, backend connection, account-registration system, or payment workflow.

1. Publisher and scope

This policy describes the current skills-only agenticintegrationhub plugin, these information pages, and related support correspondence. The publisher is Rameshkumar Varanganti. Contact contact@agenticintegrationhub.com or visit the support page for privacy questions.

Other plugins or services published by Agentic Integration Hub may have their own policies. A remote MCP service belonging to another plugin is not part of this skills-only release.

2. Information used in your AI host

Your AI host, such as ChatGPT, processes the prompts, conversations, files, and outputs you use with the skill under that provider’s terms, privacy policy, and account settings. Installing this skill does not itself send those conversations or files to the publisher.

The publisher does not receive a copy of your conversations simply because you use this skill. If you separately choose to use browsing, code execution, connected tools, or another service available in your host, that service may receive the information needed for the requested task. Those capabilities are separate from a publisher-operated connection in this plugin.

For ChatGPT, consult OpenAI’s Privacy Policy and the data controls available in your account. This policy does not replace the host provider’s policy or promise that host providers retain no data.

3. Website visits

Opening agenticintegrationhub.com is separate from using the plugin. The website is configured for Cloudflare Pages. Delivery and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, URLs, browser information, and security events as part of hosting and protecting the site.

These three information pages contain no advertising scripts, third-party analytics scripts, tracking cookies, browser-storage code, or data-submission forms. This describes the page code, not a guarantee that the hosting provider will never process technical data or use security-related mechanisms. Provider processing and retention follow the provider’s applicable practices. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

4. Support messages

If you email the publisher, the support mailbox receives your address, message, and any attachments. This information is used to respond to questions, investigate reported problems, address privacy requests, and maintain relevant support correspondence. The publisher and the email services used to deliver and store the correspondence may process it.

Support correspondence is retained in the mailbox until manually deleted; no automatic deletion schedule or fixed retention period is stated for that correspondence. Email providers may separately retain records, deleted items, or backups according to their service practices. Do not send credentials, access tokens, personal business records, or confidential customer information.

5. Sharing and external services

The current skill contains no publisher-operated mechanism for collecting, selling, or sharing your chat content, and no advertising or data-sale feature. Support information may be handled by the publisher’s email provider and technical service providers to deliver and address your inquiry, or disclosed where required by applicable law. Information you independently submit to an AI host, website, or connected service is governed by that service’s own practices.

External links take you to independently operated services. Their cookies, account requirements, processing locations, and retention may differ. Do not treat a linked service as covered by this policy.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

Use synthetic or redacted data in prompts and support requests. You may stop using or remove the skill to prevent future use through that installation. Removing it does not automatically delete host-provider records, website infrastructure records, or support correspondence.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information held by the publisher, email contact@agenticintegrationhub.com. Identify the relevant correspondence and approximate date without resending sensitive information. Reasonable identity verification may be necessary. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention requirements. Use your AI host’s controls or support for information held by that provider.

7. Intended audience and security

The plugin is intended for enterprise developers, architects, and technical users. Do not use it to submit children’s personal information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. This skills-only release does not request production credentials or require you to connect a business system.

8. Changes

This page will be updated if the plugin’s data practices change. A future publisher-operated MCP server, analytics service, sign-in flow, or payment feature would require corresponding disclosures before being represented as part of this release. Refer to the date above and the policy applicable to the version you use.
