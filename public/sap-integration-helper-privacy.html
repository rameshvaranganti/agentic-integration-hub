SAP Integration Helper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026. This policy describes the independent SAP Integration Helper plugin as currently designed. Review the disclosures before publishing.

Who operates this plugin

SAP Integration Helper is created by Rameshkumar Varanganti, through Agentic Integration Hub. It is not an official SAP or OpenAI product. For inquiries, use the contact page.

Information you may provide

When using the plugin in ChatGPT, you may submit technical questions, sample code, XML/JSON payloads, headers, configuration descriptions, or error messages. Please avoid providing passwords, tokens, personal information, confidential business records or production data. Remove sensitive details before asking for help.

How the current plugin works

The current version is skills-only, with no plugin-operated MCP server, customer SAP tenant connection, payment processing, or independently hosted AI inference service. It supplies instructions and reference content to the ChatGPT experience. Conversations and files you provide are processed through ChatGPT under the applicable OpenAI terms, privacy practices, account settings, and retention policies. Consult OpenAI’s Privacy Policy for details; this page does not replace it.

Contact and website interactions

If you contact the publisher through Agentic Integration Hub, your submitted contact information and message may be used to respond to and resolve the inquiry. Website hosting or security infrastructure may process ordinary request metadata (such as IP address and request logs) according to the site’s actual hosting configuration. This plugin policy does not set or guarantee a fixed hosting-log retention period.

Sharing, retention and deletion

The skills-only plugin does not independently sell user data, collect payments, or maintain its own database of ChatGPT conversations. The handling and retention of ChatGPT content is governed by OpenAI and the user’s relevant settings and agreements. For website contact submissions, request information or deletion through the contact page. Requests involving ChatGPT account data should be addressed through OpenAI’s controls and support.

Security and future changes

Do not provide secrets or regulated personal data in troubleshooting prompts. If this plugin later adds an MCP service, analytics, or integrations that independently receive information, this policy must be updated before those capabilities are offered. We may revise this page to reflect actual practices and applicable requirements.
