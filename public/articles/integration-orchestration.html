Orchestration

Integration Orchestration Fundamentals

Coordinate enterprise APIs, asynchronous events and dependent business processes.

Independent technical learning resource • Reviewed October 2026

Integration orchestration coordinates calls and events to complete a business process. Agents may assist with decisions or planning, but deterministic checkpoints remain useful for regulated or business-critical operations.

Design principles

  • Define transaction and failure boundaries.
  • Use correlation IDs across synchronous and asynchronous work.
  • Model compensating actions for distributed transactions.
  • Separate orchestration state from transport-specific adapters.
  • Define deadlines and dead-letter handling.
